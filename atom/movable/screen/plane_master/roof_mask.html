roof_mask
/atom/movable/screen/plane_master/roof_mask

Cutting the weather out from under a roof.

[weather_roofing.dm] stops rain doing anything to a covered tile. This stops it being drawn there, which is the other half and needs different machinery: the rain sprite is an overlay on the area, so it covers a built room whether or not anything lands in it.

Rather than fight the area overlay, roofed tiles draw their own white square on a plane nobody displays, and the weather planes take that plane as an inverse alpha mask. Weather shows where the weather mask says yes and the roof mask says nothing - so a roof punches a hole, and no upstream weather code has to know why. One white square per roofed tile, captured to a render target and shown to nobody.
