sunlight_effect
/atom/movable/sunlight_effect

The grayscale sunlight mask for one turf.

Sits in the turf's vis_contents with a null loc, the same as a lighting_object, so it can carry a per-z plane offset. White means full daylight, black means fully roofed, and anything between is a tile near a roof edge. The colour of the light is not here - the plane master tints the whole plane at once.

Spreads our light onto every corner within reach that a wall does not block.

The turf's luminosity is raised for the duration because view() will not see past darkness otherwise, and a roofed neighbour is by definition dark. It is restored before we return.

proc
stop_casting
()

Drops every corner contribution we make, and queues the turfs that lose light.

Returns immediately when we were casting nothing, which is the overwhelmingly common case: an open tile under open sky has no corners to release. Doing work here anyway queued every lit turf in the world on the first pass, and that queue was still draining minutes into the round.

Our own mask does not need queueing. The subsystem calls apply_mask on us directly after this returns.
