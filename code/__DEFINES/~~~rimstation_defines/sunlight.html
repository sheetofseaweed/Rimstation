SUNLIGHTING_PLANE

Roof-based sunlight.

A tile is dark because something is above it, not because of a flag on its area. These defines carry the plane the sunlight mask draws on, the three states a tile can be in, and the tuning for how far daylight reaches past a roof edge. Grayscale sunlight masks draw here. Relayed onto the turf lighting plate with BLEND_ADD. 13 is the only free plane between RENDER_PLANE_TURF_LIGHTING and RENDER_PLANE_EMISSIVE.

#define
SUNLIGHT_FALLOFF
(corner, source)

Distance-based strength of one sunlit tile on one corner. Corner and turf are the two arguments.

The radicand is clamped because the softening term makes it negative at short range, and sqrt of a negative is a runtime. The nearest real corner sits exactly on the boundary, so this is not a theoretical case.
