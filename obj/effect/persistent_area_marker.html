persistent_area_marker
/obj/effect/persistent_area_marker

One tile's worth of "this belonged to an area the map file cannot describe".

Records itself and goes away immediately, in the manner of every other mapping helper. Nothing reads the object; the rebuild reads what it left behind, which is what lets the marker die before the areas exist.
