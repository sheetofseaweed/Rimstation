quarian
/obj/item/clothing/gloves/quarian

Quarian gloves take a pair of ordinary gloves as a module.

Rather than hold a table of which glove grants what, the installed pair is read for its traits and insulation. Anything upstream adds to a glove therefore works here without this file being touched.

Vars

moduleThe gloves currently fitted as a module, held inside us until they are unscrewed.

Procs

apply_moduleTakes on the fitted gloves' traits, insulation and colour.
clear_modulePuts us back to a bare pair of quarian gloves.
