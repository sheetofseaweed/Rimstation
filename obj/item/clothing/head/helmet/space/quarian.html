quarian
/obj/item/clothing/head/helmet/space/quarian

The quarian helmet, which ships bare and takes two modules.

A welding mask fits behind the faceplate and a torch clips to the side. Neither is present out of the box, so a fresh helmet cannot lower its visor and has no light. The icon state carries both as "--", which is why base_icon_state is set on every colour.

Vars

torchThe torch clipped to the side, if any.
welding_maskThe welding mask fitted behind the faceplate, if any. Without it the visor will not come down.
