/obj/effect/landmark/rimstation_caravan_exitWhere a caravan can leave the colony from, and the map a colonist opens there. A mapped way out that is not the border of the map. Any turf within one tile of it counts.

/atom/movable/screen/alert/caravan_exitShown while a colonist stands where a caravan can leave from. Clicking it starts the muster.
