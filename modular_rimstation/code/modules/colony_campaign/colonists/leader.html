/datum/job/colony_leaderA colonist who can answer for the settlement. Not a head of staff: the rank is only a card the communications console accepts, so an incident asking for a reply has somebody who can give one.
/datum/outfit/job/colonist/leaderThe colonist kit, plus the hat that says who was asked.
/datum/id_trim/job/colony_leaderThe colonist trim plus ACCESS_COMMAND. Deliberately a superset: applying a trim clears the card first, so a colonist promoted mid-campaign must not lose access on the way.
/proc/stamp_colony_leader_accessWrites a leader's rank onto the card they are actually carrying. Returns TRUE if it changed one.
