/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgenGenerator version 2: land that comes in regions, and campaigns that keep the generator they began with.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/land_comes_in_regionsNeighbouring cells share a biome far more often than with one roll per hex.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/home_valley_is_openThe colony starts in open country, whatever the planet and the roughness.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/water_is_low_and_impassableWater fills the low ground, cannot be crossed, and sits below every land cell.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/peaks_form_rangesPeaks stand in ranges with foothills, not one at a time on the plain.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/roughness_changes_the_goingRougher settings give more hard going and more peaks.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/ranges_leave_a_passRanges never wall land off from the colony. A pass is cut instead; only water can make an island.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/version_one_is_unchangedThe first generator still builds exactly what it always built, for campaigns made with it.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/overworld_keeps_its_generatorA campaign's world is rebuilt by the generator it was made with, until an admin moves it on.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/climate_follows_the_planetThe planet's climate decides the region's: a cold planet is mostly cold country, a temperate one mostly mild.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_worldgen/danger_rises_with_distanceEvery danger rating appears, and the far country is more dangerous than the colony's own.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/overworld_map_feeds_the_globeThe map sends what the globe draws: the planet, the time of day, and each known hex's height.
