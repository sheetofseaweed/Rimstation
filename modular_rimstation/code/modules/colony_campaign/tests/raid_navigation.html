/proc/rimstation_closest_approachLets walker move on its own for up to timeout, and returns the closest it came to target.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_raider_reaches_coreA raider sent at the core has to arrive next to it, with its real AI doing the walking and no player near. Unwatched off-station AI sleeps, and JPS cannot end on the dense core tile. Either one left raiders standing.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_raider_follows_new_legA raider handed a new leg mid-walk has to walk to the new leg, not the old one. The JPS move loop kept its first target, so raiders shuffled at their first leg on the real map.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_maps_build_in_backgroundRoute maps the raid queues have to finish on their own, in slices, with nobody waiting on them.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_thief_reaches_stashA thief has to reach a stash to loot it, and a stash is as dense as the core.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_raider_reroutesA raider closing on its leg is left alone, and one that lost its leg gets a new one from where it stands. Combat drags raiders off their legs, and JPS refuses any leg further than AI_MAX_PATH_LENGTH.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_retreat_keeps_walkingA retreat has to hand out its later legs, or it stops at the first waypoint and the raid never ends.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_time_limitsOne stuck raider must not hold a raid open, because a running raid blocks every later raid and caravan.
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_single_entry_edgeA raid arrives from the one direction it announces, and leaves the same way.
