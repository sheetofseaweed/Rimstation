/proc/get_colony_larderThe larder the colony stocks, or null if nobody has built one.
/proc/food_units_inWhat one item is worth as stored food.
/proc/count_colony_foodHow much food the colony is holding, counted from the larder itself.
/proc/sync_colony_food_to_ledgerMakes the ledger's food figure equal what is actually in the larder.
/proc/consume_colony_foodTakes food out of the larder. Returns TRUE only if the whole amount was there.
/proc/cmp_food_units_ascSmallest first, so spending food reaches for the scraps before the loaves.
/proc/colony_food_shortfall_priceWhat buying in the shortfall would cost, in credits.
/proc/pay_for_colony_foodPays for units of food however the colony can: out of the larder first, then out of its budget.
/proc/colony_food_problemWhy the colony cannot put this much food together, or null if it can.
/proc/return_colony_foodGives food back to the colony. Returns TRUE if it went somewhere.
