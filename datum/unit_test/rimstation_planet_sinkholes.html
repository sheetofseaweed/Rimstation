rimstation_planet_sinkholes
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_planet_sinkholes

A landscape has to open a few ways down into the underground, and every one of them has to be a two-way route.

The failure this guards is a hole nobody can climb out of. Stairs lift a climber to the tile past them, so an exit facing the wrong way puts the top of the climb over open air and drops them straight back in - and the only sign of that in play is a colonist who went down and could not come back.
