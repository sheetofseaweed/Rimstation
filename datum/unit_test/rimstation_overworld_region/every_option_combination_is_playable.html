every_option_combination_is_playable
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_overworld_region/every_option_combination_is_playable

Every combination of the three creation options builds a region somebody can actually play.

Twenty-seven worlds, and a player can pick any of them at creation. Each has to be bounded, connected enough to leave the colony, and stable - a combination that generated an unreachable starter deposit, or a different region on each rebuild, would be a campaign that could not be played and would not say so until somebody had already committed to it.
