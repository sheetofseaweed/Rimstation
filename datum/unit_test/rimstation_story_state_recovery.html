rimstation_story_state_recovery
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_story_state_recovery

Recovery is what the colony is owed, and it is only paid back by quiet chapters that went well.

The distinction being tested is between loss, which is a memory of the last thing and fades on its own, and recovery, which is a debt. If recovery fell for any surviving chapter, a colony that wins every raid would be treated as though nothing had been happening to it.
