Nobody is ever handed a destination they cannot path to.

Basic-mob JPS refuses any path longer than AI_MAX_PATH_LENGTH. A raider given a target beyond that does not walk as far as it can and stop - it stands exactly still, which reads in game as broken pathfinding and is how this was found twice. Every destination therefore has to arrive as a chain of short legs.
