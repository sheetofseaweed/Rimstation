rimstation_raid_stands_down_on_resolve
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_stands_down_on_resolve

A finished raid stops marching.

Survivors keep their own AI and will still defend themselves, but they stop walking at an objective that is no longer theirs to take. It also stops queued pathfinding outliving the fight - a travel destination is a turf, move loops do not watch turfs for deletion, and a generation reset deletes every turf on the map.
