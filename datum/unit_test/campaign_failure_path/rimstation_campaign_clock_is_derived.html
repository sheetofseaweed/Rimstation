rimstation_campaign_clock_is_derived
/datum/unit_test/campaign_failure_path/rimstation_campaign_clock_is_derived

Campaign time has to be one number, and it has to be the same number everywhere.

The clock is derived from two immutable origins rather than counted up, so these drive the real lifecycle - chapters starting, snapshots, commits, defeat, a new generation - and read the clock the way the rest of the campaign reads it. Elapsed time is simulated by moving an origin backwards rather than by sleeping, because what is under test is the derivation, and a test that waited two hours of world time to prove it would take two hours.
