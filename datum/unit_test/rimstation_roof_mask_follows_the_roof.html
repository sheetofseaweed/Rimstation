The hole in the weather follows the roof.

Only the bookkeeping is checked here - whether a tile is wearing its mask, and whether that mask is on the plane the weather subtracts. Whether the rain then stops being drawn is a question for a client's eyes; what this can prove is that the right tiles are marked and no others, which is where the cost would run away.
