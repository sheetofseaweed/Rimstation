rimstation_raid_arrival_is_not_extraction
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_arrival_is_not_extraction

Landing on the edge is not the same as leaving by it.

Attackers arrive on insertion turfs, and insertion turfs are edge tiles by definition - that is how they are validated. So a theft raid that treats "standing on the edge" as departure deletes its own attackers on the first tick, empty-handed, on the ground they just landed on, and resolves as repelled before anybody has moved. Departure means carrying something, or having been called off.
