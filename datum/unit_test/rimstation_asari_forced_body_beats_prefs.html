The forced body beats the preferences, which apply after the species one.

PREFERENCE_PRIORITY_SPECIES is 3, gender is 5 and body type is 6, so anything set during on_species_gain() is overwritten moments later. apply_supplementary_body_changes() runs after every preference, which is the only reason this holds. Setting both back by hand here stands in for those two preferences having just applied.
