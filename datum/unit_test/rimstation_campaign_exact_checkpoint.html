rimstation_campaign_exact_checkpoint
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_campaign_exact_checkpoint

A named checkpoint is loaded, or nothing is.

The inherited selection walks every save on disk and takes the newest loadable one, which is precisely how a town from a previous generation comes back after a defeat. A campaign names its checkpoint, and a named checkpoint that fails validation has to be a hard failure rather than a licence to load something else.
