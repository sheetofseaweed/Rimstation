rimstation_incidents_not_destroyed_before_roundstart
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_incidents_not_destroyed_before_roundstart

No incident is destroyed before the round begins.

The tag index builds one of every incident type and throws them away, and it used to do that while the global lists were still being built. The garbage collector stamps a queue entry with the world.time it was made at, and create_and_destroy waits for the queue to drain past its own start time - so an entry stamped with almost nothing is one it can never wait out. Seven of them cost that test its whole 50 minute budget.
