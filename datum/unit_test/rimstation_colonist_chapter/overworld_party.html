overworld_party
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/overworld_party

Forming an expedition, and refusing to form a broken one.

A party is the only thing in this campaign that takes colonists off the map, so the interesting cases are all refusals: somebody who is dead, somebody who was never here, somebody signed on twice, or a plan that changed after people agreed to it. These build a real campaign with real bodies rather than calling the membership rules directly, because every one of those rules is about the state of somebody's body.
