death_outlives_absence
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/death_outlives_absence

A colonist who was away for a chapter is not resurrected, and a dead one is not brought back by absence.

The away rule sweeps everybody the chapter did not see, so it has to be the one rule that never touches the dead. Otherwise a colony would forget its dead simply by playing a chapter without them - which is every chapter after the one they died in.
