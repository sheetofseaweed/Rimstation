hunger_stops_departure
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/overworld_party/hunger_stops_departure

A colony that cannot feed an expedition does not send one - but an empty larder alone does not stop it.

Two different situations that would look the same from a bare "there is no food" check: a colony with no stores but money in the bank buys rations in, and only a colony with neither is actually stuck.
