stash_declares_worn_items
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/stash_declares_worn_items

A colonist's belongings are declared for saving while they are still wearing them.

The whole design rests on this: carbons are excluded from the map save, so worn items are deleted every chapter unless something else claims them. Declaring rather than packing is what lets that happen without anybody being undressed by a commit, an autosave, or an admin's mid-round snapshot.
