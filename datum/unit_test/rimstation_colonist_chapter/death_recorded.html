death_recorded
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/death_recorded

The colony remembers that somebody died, and remembers it from the moment it happened.

Death has to be recorded when it happens rather than worked out at the end of the chapter. A body that was gibbed, dusted or otherwise deleted is not there to be inspected at commit time, so a colony that waited until then would quietly lose exactly the deaths that mattered most.
