skills_captured_at_commit
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_chapter/skills_captured_at_commit

A colonist's skills reach their record whether or not their body survives the chapter.

Capture at commit can only read bodies that are still standing. Somebody who logged off, or who was gibbed, would otherwise donate a chapter of learning to nobody - so the binding writes their skills down as it is torn off, and the commit is a sweep of whoever is still here rather than the only chance to be recorded.
