rimstation_asari_keeps_sexes
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_asari_keeps_sexes

The asari keep sexes = TRUE, and that is deliberate.

Turning it off looks like the obvious way to express a mono-gender race, and does the opposite here: it drops the female uniform shaping and the dimorphic chest sprite, so an all-feminine species would render with the masculine silhouette. The two locks are what express it instead.
