/datum/unit_test/rimstation_sunlight_boundary_never_in_the_past

Every step is reachable, including the one that wraps past midnight.

The wrap is the case the boundary arithmetic gets wrong: the first step of the day starts at fraction zero, which has already gone past by the time the last step is running, so a naive "when does the next step start" lands in the past and the clock advances on every tick for the rest of the day.
