/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colonist_skills_round_trip

A colonist carries what they learned into the next chapter.

This is the payload of the whole phase. Preferences already carry a character's name and face between rounds, and the other players remember who did what - skill is the one thing a colonist genuinely cannot bring back with them, so it is the one thing worth writing down.

Restoring goes through set_experience() rather than assigning the numbers, because levels, traits and the status effects some skills grant on level-up are all derived from experience by that call. A colonist who came back with the right number and the wrong level would be worse than one who came back untrained.
