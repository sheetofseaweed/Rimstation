/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colony_larder

The colony's food store: the one thing that puts food into the campaign.

Before the larder existed, every use of food was a spend and nothing anywhere was a source, so the figure sat at zero forever and anything gated on it could never happen. These are mostly about the two rules that keeps honest: the larder is what the number counts, and spending is all or nothing.
