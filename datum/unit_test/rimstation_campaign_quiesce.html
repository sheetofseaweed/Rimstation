The world has to stop changing while a checkpoint is written.

A checkpoint is produced by walking the map. Anything that mutates it mid-walk yields a save showing half the town before an event and half after - a state that never existed. The guard is asserted at its call sites, not just where it is defined, because a quiesce nobody consults is decoration.
