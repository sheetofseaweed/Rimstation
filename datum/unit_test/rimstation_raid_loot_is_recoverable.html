/datum/unit_test/rimstation_raid_loot_is_recoverable

Loot only counts once it has left the map.

This is the rule the whole objective rests on. A raider standing in your settlement with its arms full has taken nothing yet - kill it and the goods fall on the floor, still yours. Without that, theft would be something that simply happens to a colony rather than something it can fight.
