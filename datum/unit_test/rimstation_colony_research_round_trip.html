rimstation_colony_research_round_trip
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_colony_research_round_trip

A colony keeps what it researched, and keeps only what it actually paid for.

The techweb is rebuilt from nothing every round, so without this a campaign would begin each chapter having forgotten everything. Restoring is not a purchase: a colony that already paid for a node must not be charged again, and the balance it comes back with has to be the balance it left with.
