rimstation_sunlight_clock_advances
/datum/unit_test/rimstation_sunlight_clock_advances

The day actually moves on, and does not then move on again every tick.

should_advance_time() is a cached boundary rather than a recalculation, because it runs on every tick of the round. A cache like that has two failure modes and the test beside this one only catches the second: a boundary that never arrives freezes the sky at whatever it was, and a boundary that is not pushed forward on arrival re-advances for ever, rerolling the sky colour every tick. Both are pinned here.
