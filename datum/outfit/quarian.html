quarian
/datum/outfit/quarian

A quarian spawns in their own enviro-suit instead of the job's uniform.

pre_equip_species_outfit() runs before the job outfit, and the job's own uniform, gloves, shoes and head are then deleted on arrival because those slots are already full. That is the same mechanism plasmamen use for their envirosuit.

The source build carried one outfit datum per job and a quarian_outfit var on every /datum/job. This reads the department off the job instead, so only the handful of jobs whose colour does not follow their department need naming.
