midnight
/datum/time_of_day/midnight

Full night, and the only genuinely dark step.

Starts at 21:00 rather than 20:00 so that night runs exactly eight hours, 21:00 to 05:00. The hour it gave up went to dusk and the half hour at the far end to dawn, so the extra time is daylight rather than a longer twilight on one side only.
