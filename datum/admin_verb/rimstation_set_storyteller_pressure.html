rimstation_set_storyteller_pressure
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_set_storyteller_pressure

Reads and sets the two numbers that decide how hard the colony's next chapter is.

Loss is what just went wrong and fades on its own; recovery is how much easier the colony is owed and only comes down through quiet chapters. They are the storyteller's whole memory of the campaign, so being able to read them is most of the value here - a colony being handed nothing but disasters, or nothing at all, is explained by these two numbers and by nothing else.
