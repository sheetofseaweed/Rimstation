rimstation_inspect_colony_raid
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_inspect_colony_raid

Reports what the running raid is actually doing.

A raid is mostly invisible from outside: how many attackers are left, how far the core has been taken and what the thing is even trying to do are all on the datum. Asking beforehand is how you decide whether to end it, so this reads and changes nothing.
