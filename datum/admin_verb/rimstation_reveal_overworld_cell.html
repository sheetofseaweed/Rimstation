rimstation_reveal_overworld_cell
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_reveal_overworld_cell

Debug tools for driving an expedition without waiting for one.

These exist because a caravan takes real minutes to cross real ground, and one person testing alone cannot sit through a full journey to check the third thing that happens on it. Every one of them logs, and none is reachable from anything a player touches - they are a way to reach a state, not a way to play. Puts one hex on the map without anybody walking to it.
