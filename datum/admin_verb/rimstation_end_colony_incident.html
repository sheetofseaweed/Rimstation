rimstation_end_colony_incident
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_end_colony_incident

Stops a running incident, either as cancelled or with a real outcome.

The two are not interchangeable. Cancelling says nothing happened: reservations go back and the pacing state learns nothing, which is what you want for an incident that fired by mistake. Resolving says the colony met it and this is how it went, which is recorded and shapes what the storyteller offers next.
