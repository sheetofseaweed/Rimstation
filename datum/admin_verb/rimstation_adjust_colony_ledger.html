rimstation_adjust_colony_ledger
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_adjust_colony_ledger

Moves the settlement's money or materials, and says in the ledger that an admin did it.

Goes through the ordinary mutators rather than writing the balance directly, so the bank account stays the authority on what the colony can afford and the movement leaves the same audit trail as any other. An adjustment nobody can find afterwards is worse than no adjustment: the ledger is what a later chapter reads to explain where the money went.
