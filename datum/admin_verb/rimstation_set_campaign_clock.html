rimstation_set_campaign_clock
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_set_campaign_clock

Moves the campaign clock.

The clock is derived from two origins rather than accumulated, so it cannot be nudged by writing a total - moving it means moving the origin it counts from. Everything that stamps a moment reads this, so a clock dragged backwards past entries that already exist would put the campaign's history out of order. Only forward moves are offered for that reason.
