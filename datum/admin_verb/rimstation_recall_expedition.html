rimstation_recall_expedition
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_recall_expedition

Brings an expedition home from wherever it is, intact.

The recovery tool rather than a gameplay one: it exists for a party that has ended up somewhere the game cannot get it out of. It walks them home properly rather than teleporting, so the return, the refund and the arrival all happen the way they normally would.
