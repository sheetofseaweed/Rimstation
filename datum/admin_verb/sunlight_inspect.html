sunlight_inspect
/datum/admin_verb/sunlight_inspect

Dumps what the sunlight system thinks about one tile.

The three layers fail differently and look the same on screen: a tile can be wrongly roofed, correctly roofed but wrongly rendered, or correct but stuck in a queue. This says which.
