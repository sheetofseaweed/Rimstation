rimstation_declare_colony_defeat
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_declare_colony_defeat

Closes the generation without pretending the chapter resolved.

Separate from ending a chapter as a failure, because the two are different claims. A failed chapter is a result the colony earned and the pacing state learns from. This is an admin saying the generation is over regardless - for a colony wrecked by a bug, or one abandoned - and it goes straight to the closure.
