rimstation_reveal_overworld_region
/datum/admin_verb/rimstation_reveal_overworld_region

Reveals the whole region, or everything within a radius of a cell.

The existing single-cell verb reveals one hex and its neighbours, which is the right shape for testing a discovery but the wrong one for setting up a scenario. Revealing the map is the common case by a long way.
