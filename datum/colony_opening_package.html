colony_opening_package
/datum/colony_opening_package

What a colony is given to start with.

The opening has to be survivable for two players and still not hand twenty players an industrial base, so quantities are base + ceil(colonists / per_colonist) clamped to a cap. Shared supplies are granted once for the settlement; personal supplies are granted per colonist.

The contents are deliberately pre-industrial. Anything that removes the early production chain - a lathe, an RCD, energy weapons - belongs to progression, not to the first ten minutes.
