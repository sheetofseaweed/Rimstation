storm
/datum/colony_incident/storm

Weather the colony has to be indoors for.

The whole incident is a wrapper around /datum/weather, which already telegraphs, runs, winds down and spares anyone under a roof. That is exactly the shape wanted here - a warning worth acting on, and a reward for having built somewhere to shelter - so nothing about it is reimplemented.

Which storm arrives comes from the planet's own weather_set, so an arid world throws sand and a cold one throws snow. That field has been in the planet record since Phase 1 and nothing read it until now.
