dispute
/datum/colony_incident/dispute

The settlement disagrees with itself, and somebody has to rule on it.

Two colonists, one dispute, two rulings. Whoever is ruled against takes it badly and whoever is ruled for takes it well, both through the mood system the game already has - so the consequence is carried by the people involved rather than by an abstract number.

Refusing to rule is a third answer in practice: the decision expires, both parties stay aggrieved, and the settlement carries the pressure.
