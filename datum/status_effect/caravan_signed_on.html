caravan_signed_on
/datum/status_effect/caravan_signed_on

The reminder that you have signed on to an expedition.

Exists because signing on happens at a table and departing happens at a post, which can be a long way apart and a long time later. Without it, the commonest way for a caravan to fail to leave is one person having forgotten they were on it.
