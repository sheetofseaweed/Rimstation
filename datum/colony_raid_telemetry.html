colony_raid_telemetry
/datum/colony_raid_telemetry

The record of what one raid actually did.

Balance for a colony raid cannot be reasoned about from the budget alone - what matters is whether the warning was long enough, whether the composition that arrived matched what was paid for, and what the settlement looked like afterwards. All of that has to be captured while it happens.

Settlement damage is measured against a list of structures registered at warning time rather than by scanning the world at resolution. Scanning would be both expensive and wrong: it cannot tell a wall the raiders broke from a wall the colonists never built.
