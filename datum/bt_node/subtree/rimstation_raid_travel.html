rimstation_raid_travel
/datum/bt_node/subtree/rimstation_raid_travel

Raider AI: fight what is in front of you, otherwise walk at the colony.

This is deliberately a thin layer over the inherited trooper AI. Local combat, obstacle breaking and target selection already work; the only thing missing for a raid is somewhere to go when nothing is shooting at them. Without that they arrive and mill around at the map edge, which is exactly what happened.

The inherited trees do carry a travel subtree, but it is keyed on BB_BASIC_MOB_REINFORCEMENT_TARGET and clears itself on arrival. This one reads BB_TRAVEL_DESTINATION and keeps it, so attackers that get pushed off the objective turn around and come back rather than wandering.

It sits where upstream's trooper trees put random_walk: below every combat branch, so a raider fights what is in front of it and only walks at the colony when nothing is.
