roof_mask_weather
/datum/preference/toggle/roof_mask_weather

Whether this client cuts the rain out from under roofs.

Correctness costs a render pass. The mask is a plane captured to a render target and subtracted from the weather planes as an inverse alpha filter, which is a second full-screen mask on top of the one upstream already runs for particle weather. On a machine that was only just keeping up, that is the kind of thing that turns rain into a slideshow.

Off, the tiles keep their masks - those are turf overlays, shared by everybody - and this client simply stops subtracting them. Rain is then drawn over roofed ground again, exactly as it was before the mask existed. It still does not do anything there: [weather_roofing.dm] is what keeps a sheltered colonist dry, and it is server-side and unaffected.

Defaults to on. A preference exists at all because "is this what is slow" cannot be answered from a log - it needs the player who is complaining to turn one thing off and say whether it helped.
