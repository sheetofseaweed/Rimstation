campaign_skill_decay_per_chapter
/datum/config_entry/number/campaign_skill_decay_per_chapter

How much of a colonist's experience is lost each chapter, as a proportion.

The counterweight to carrying skills between chapters: without one, a colonist only ratchets upward, since nothing in a campaign takes skill away. Ships at 0 - the mechanism exists, switched off - because the right rate cannot be judged before several chapters have actually been played. 0.1 costs a tenth per chapter.
