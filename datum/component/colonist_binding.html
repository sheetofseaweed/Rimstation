colonist_binding
/datum/component/colonist_binding

Marks a body as the one playing a particular colonist this chapter.

A component rather than a var on the mob, for two reasons. It cleans itself up when the body is deleted, which matters because a colonist's body can be gibbed, dusted or otherwise removed mid-chapter and the campaign must not be left holding a reference to it. And it keeps the hook out of inherited types entirely - nothing upstream has to know that colonists exist.

The binding is deliberately one-directional in importance: losing it costs the colony knowledge of which body was whose, never the colonist record itself. People outlive their bodies here.
