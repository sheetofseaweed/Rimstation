quarian_suit_integrity
/datum/component/quarian_suit_integrity

Watches a quarian's enviro-suit and makes them ill while it is open.

One component on the mob rather than hooks on each garment: the source build copy-pasted the same pickup/dropped/equipped bodies into all four, and its infection timer rescheduled itself forever, surviving death and species change. This starts and stops with the seal and dies with its mob.

Vars

exposureSeconds of open suit banked since the last infection roll.
sealedWhether all four pieces were on at the last check.
