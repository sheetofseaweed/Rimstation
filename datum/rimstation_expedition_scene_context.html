rimstation_expedition_scene_context
/datum/rimstation_expedition_scene_context

Local terrain plan and runtime materializer for one expedition scene.

All generation works in local coordinates. Moving the reservation, loading another scene first, or changing world.maxx therefore cannot change the result. The strategic cell supplies its visible terrain, traversal topology, and danger; the planet and stable site id supply the random streams.
