overworld_scene_provider
/datum/overworld_scene_provider

Runtime scene providers for expeditions.

A provider owns how a scene is made, while /datum/overworld_destination continues to own the resulting reservation and the landmarks found in it. This keeps the expedition lifecycle independent of content: transit stays authored, resource landscapes are generated, and ruins combine terrain with authored structures.
