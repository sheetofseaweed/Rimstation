time_of_day
/datum/time_of_day

One step of the day, and the colour the sky takes during it.

Colours are lists so a step can vary between days - two dawns that look identical every single round read as a loop rather than a world. One is picked when the step begins.

Vars

coloursColours this step may take. One is picked per occurrence.
startWhen this step starts, as a fraction of the day from 0 to 1.
